### T-370..T-374: TUI TaskPanel
- `aethercode-tui/src/components/TaskPanel.tsx` (R321) — presentational
  React/Ink component. Lists every supervised child (status icon +

  `a` attach, `k` kill, `r` resume, `x` retry, `j/k` or arrows to
  navigate, `Esc` to close, `Enter` for attach.
